## Why "Hello, World"?

Almost every programming language starts its first lesson with the same sentence.
It's short, it's decisive: if those words appear on screen, the whole path from
your environment to the toolchain works.

Blogging is roughly the same. You don't need a positioning statement, a column
structure, or a publishing schedule — you just need the first post to exist, and
everything else has somewhere to grow from.
